Option Brand visible? Header height Button labels

My recommendation: Option 1 OR Hybrid (1 + 4)
Option 1 (Above row) gives the cleanest, most professional look — your brand always visible without crowding any button. The +28px header bump is well worth it.

Even better: Combine 1 + 4 — brand appears in both the header AND inside the search sheet. Maximum reinforcement, zero downside.
